Founder's Welcome Note

Welcome to the Kivulini Moot and Mock Trial Club!

The Kivulini Moot and Mock Trial Club at Tumaini University Makumira (TUMA) provides a unique platform for aspiring legal professionals to bridge the gap between classroom theory and courtroom reality. We offer an environment conducive for the realization of each student’s advocacy dreams, focusing on the rigorous development of research, oral presentation, and strategic litigation skills.

Our mission is driven by the pursuit of legal excellence and the translation of knowledge into meaningful solutions that improve our society. Whether you are interested in the complexities of the Corporate Law, the nuances of Criminal Procedure, or the evolving landscape of International Law, our club is the place where you can thrive.

It is my privilege to serve as the Founder and Coordinator of this organization, which devotes itself to nurturing your unique skills and preparing you to serve the legal system anywhere and everywhere. By joining us, you are engaging with a community committed to academic excellence and professional growth.

You have chosen the right path to sharpen your legal mind. You have chosen to be a leader of tomorrow!

Elisha Patrick Francis
Founder & Coordinator
